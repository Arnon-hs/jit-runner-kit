# Maintainers

## Current maintainers

- [@Arnon-hs](https://github.com/Arnon-hs) — project lead, releases, security, and final decisions.

The project currently has a bus factor of one. That is a known pre-1.0 risk, not an implied guarantee of continuous support.

## Responsibilities

- Triage issues and pull requests on a best-effort basis.
- Preserve runner isolation, credential boundaries, and cleanup behavior.
- Keep release and compatibility notes accurate.
- Coordinate private vulnerability response.
- Document significant decisions publicly.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainers are invited based on sustained, constructive contributions; sound security and operational judgment; respectful collaboration; and willingness to share maintenance work. There is no contribution-count threshold or paid path to maintainership.
